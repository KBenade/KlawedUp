Who we are

KlawedUp is an emerging nail brand specializing in high-quality, handmade press-on nails. The business was founded out of a passion for nail art and creativity, with the goal of providing stylish, affordable, and reusable nail sets for individuals who want salon-quality nails from the comfort of their homes.

The brand focuses on combining convenience with aesthetics, offering a variety of trendy and custom-designed press-on nails suitable for different occasions and personal styles.

Our Mission

At KlawedUp, our mission is to empower individuals to express their unique style through our high-quality, handmade press-on nails.

We are committed to providing stylish, affordable, and reusable nail sets that allow our customers to achieve salon-quality nails from the comfort of their homes. Our goal is to inspire creativity and confidence in every individual.

To provide high-quality, stylish, and reusable press-on nails that allow customers to express their individuality with convenience and confidence

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the leading brand for high-quality, handmade press-on nails, recognized for our innovative designs and commitment to customer satisfaction.

To grow KlawedUp into a recognized beauty brand known for creativity, quality, and innovation in the press-on nail industry.
